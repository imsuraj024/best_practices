# Architecture Decision Record

Use an ADR when a decision has meaningful technical trade-offs or will be expensive to reverse.

## Title

Short decision name.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Deprecated

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and relevant alternatives.

## Decision

State the chosen approach clearly.

## Alternatives considered

List the realistic alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Document benefits, costs, risks, and operational impact.

## Review trigger

State what would cause the team to revisit the decision.
